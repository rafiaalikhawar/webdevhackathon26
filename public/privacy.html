Privacy Policy

RESX · Last updated 18 October 2026

RESX is a customer relationship management web application that helps businesses respond to their customers, track sales and book appointments. This policy explains what information RESX handles and why.

Information we collect

  • Account information: name, email address and a securely hashed password for people who sign up.
  • Customer records entered by a business: names, phone numbers, email addresses, notes, deals and appointments.
  • Messages: when a business connects WhatsApp, the messages customers send to that business and the replies the business sends, together with delivery status.
  • Payment status: whether a payment request was paid, failed or refunded, with the payment provider's reference. We never see or store card numbers.
  • Calendar data: when an employee connects Google Calendar, busy times and the appointments created from RESX.

How we use it

We use this information only to provide the service: to show conversations and customer records to the right people in a business, to assign and follow up on leads, to send messages the business writes, to collect payments and to schedule appointments. We do not sell personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

Who can see it

Each business's data is kept separate from every other business. Within a business, access depends on the permissions its owner grants. Information is shared with the services a business chooses to connect (WhatsApp by Meta, the payment provider and Google Calendar) only as needed to perform the actions it requested.

Storage and security

Data is stored in a managed PostgreSQL database. Connection credentials for third-party services are encrypted, and passwords are stored only as salted hashes. Access to data is enforced on the server.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept while the business uses RESX. A business or an individual customer can ask us to delete their data at any time by emailing the address below. We will act on verified requests within 30 days.

Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you. WhatsApp customers can opt out of messages by replying STOP.
